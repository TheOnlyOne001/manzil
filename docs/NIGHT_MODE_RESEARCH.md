# Night Walk: Chennai research and proposed implementation
Research date: 2026-11-06. Planning only; no night-routing feature installed.

## Product
Prefer walkable, better-lit and more observable corridors within a user-selected detour/time/walking budget. Women-centred co-design, available to anyone. Do not label routes guaranteed safe or derive safety from gender or neighbourhood stereotypes. Broader carriageway is not pedestrian space. Never route onto a pedestrian-prohibited highway/flyover, across a railway, through a locked campus gate or inaccessible staircase to obtain a higher comfort score.

## Verified local data
148,961 highway ways; lit 1,127 (1,064 yes, 62 no, one limited), width 147, lanes 3,452, sidewalk 334 (291 no; 43 explicit positive), foot 684, surface 9,303. These are way counts, not distance coverage. The transport-filtered source has only one ATM among selected police/pharmacy/hospital/fuel/ATM categories, so a separate POI ingestion is essential. Missing tags mean unknown, not unlit/unsafe. Wider-road proxy alone cannot support claims like 94% well lit.

Bundled BRouter lookup encodes lit, lanes, sidewalk, foot, access, tunnel, service, maxspeed. Width and opening_hours are absent. Merely mentioning width in a brf cannot recover it from existing rd5. First validate tag presence through routed debug output. External streetwidth, lamp observations and time-aware POIs need an enrichment graph/edge index or candidate scoring; a full scoring search requires an engine extension or rebuilt graph.

## Routing design
Hard filters first: pedestrian access, gates, crossing legality, wheelchair/stair constraints, current closures. Then bounded additive edge costs in equivalent time: walking time + dark exposure + long contiguous low-observation stretches + crossing/traffic burden + unknown-data penalty. Positive evidence can reduce a bounded penalty; do not multiply many correlated bonuses or let cost become negative. Compare candidate routes under a detour budget, e.g. +5 or +10 minutes subject to maximum walking distance. These are preference defaults, not scientifically calibrated safety weights.

Generate candidates inside our routable graph (normal walking, lighting-weighted, walkpath/crossing-weighted), deduplicate and score in a time-dependent pass. Alternative APIs may return fewer candidates and never offer arbitrary custom edge costs; reranking cannot find a corridor absent from candidates. If no candidate improves evidence, say so rather than invent a safer route. Include longest unknown/dark stretch and evidence coverage by distance, not just average score. For full dynamic graph search, preprocess a consistent topology with original OSM way IDs and sidecar segment attributes; do not just draw a nicer line.

Evaluate POI availability at predicted segment arrival, and connect to a real accessible entrance on the same pedestrian network. Do not reward a hospital across a six-lane road, a pharmacy behind a closed gate or the assumption that every branch is 24/7. Police/CCTV locations are contextual information, not automatic safety bonuses. Keep sources, observed_at, fetched_at, expiry and confidence separate.

## Frontend and agent
Moon toggle Night Walk. Keep route blue; add restrained warm-gold lighting accents; use dashed slate for unknown. Small support-place icons only on relevant corridor. A short decision strip states extra minutes and the main factual reason; tap opens source and age. Separate Lighting / Open places / Recent reports in Layers. Panda explains a verified tradeoff or changed condition once; it does not repeatedly announce safe. Voice examples: Prefer lit roads, avoid underpasses, I can walk five extra minutes, take me to a nearby open staffed place. LLM parses bounded preferences and explains computed evidence; deterministic routing owns access and geometry. Offline voice availability is device-dependent; buttons always work.

Optional trusted-contact sharing/check-in must be explicitly enabled, expiry-limited and not publish a user's live route to strangers. Offline route remains available, but live sharing cannot be claimed without connectivity. Reports should concern concrete conditions (lamp out, gate closed, harassment incident), be privacy-preserving/moderated and time-limited, not classify people as suspicious by appearance.

## First real pilot
Audit two alternative corridors for an actual SRM/Potheri origin/destination and one central Chennai last-mile pair with local women commuters/campus security. Map entrances, light function, footpaths, crossings and operating windows. Do not ask someone to enter an isolated route for testing. First prove the alternative is connected and usable. Compare normal and Night Walk routes with real extra time, observed light coverage and longest unknown stretch. Then test gate closures, expiring lamp reports, disabled GPS, airplane cold start and maximum-walk limits. Automated adverse-condition fixtures must be labelled tests, never installed as live reports. No claim of reduced assaults or crime probability follows from a successful route test.
